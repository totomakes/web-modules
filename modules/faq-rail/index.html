FAQ

We have the answers

Keep scrolling. The section below pins itself and steps to the next category each screenful, so the FAQ keeps going instead of ending after one list.

What makes this different from a normal dashboard?
It is built for decisions, not reporting. Instead of only showing what happened, it tells you what is about to happen and what to do about it.
How does the AI help, in plain English?
It categorises and summarises activity, highlights what is unusual, and drafts the next action so you approve rather than assemble.
Who is it for?
Founders and small teams who need a finance view without hiring an analyst to maintain it.
Is there a free trial?
Fourteen days, no card. Your data stays yours if you leave.
How far ahead can it forecast?
Thirteen weeks by default, extendable to twelve months once there is enough history to be honest about the error bars.
What happens with irregular revenue?
Seasonality and one-offs are separated from the baseline, so a single large invoice does not distort the trend.
Can I model scenarios?
Yes. Change a hire date, a price, or a payment term and the projection re-runs against the same history.
How long does setup take?
About ten minutes. Connect your bank and accounting tool, and the first forecast lands while you are still reading this.
Which tools connect?
The usual suspects for banking, accounting and billing, plus a webhook for anything that is not on the list.
Can I import history?
Upload a CSV and it is mapped automatically, with a review step before anything is committed.
Does it work with multiple entities?
Yes, consolidated or side by side, with per-entity permissions.
Where is my data stored?
Encrypted at rest and in transit, in the region you pick at sign-up. Nothing leaves it without you asking.
Do you train models on my numbers?
No. Your data is never used to train shared models, and that is in the contract, not just the marketing.
Can I delete everything?
One button, and the export runs first so you keep a copy.
